NCL has three versions. They are not levels of severity, but directions. Adopters should choose the version that matches their intent.

The standard version, suitable for the vast majority of cases.

1. Non-commercial use, modification, and distribution are free of charge.
2. Commercial use requires explicit written authorization from the original author.
3. The original author retains all commercial rights.

### (2) NCL Pro

For those who do not wish the work to be used commercially by any party, including the original author.

## 4. How to Adopt

1. Choose the applicable version.
2. Publish the corresponding license file together with the work. The form of the work is not limited; source code, video, audio, documents, images, datasets, model weights, and others are all acceptable.
3. The adopter should indicate the adopted version at the place where the work is published or within the work itself.
4. The adopter should provide valid contact information at the place where the work is published or within the work itself. If the work is distributed as a packaged file, a file containing the contact information should be placed inside the package.
5. If additional terms are needed, a separate additional terms statement should be made, which must not conflict with the core terms of NCL. The form of the additional terms statement is not limited; it may be a file, a video description, metadata, publication page text, or accompanying documentation, as long as it can be clearly accessed by recipients of the work.

The license file must remain unmodified. The copyright notice of the original author must not be removed or obscured.
